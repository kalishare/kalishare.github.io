Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains how Jourify Keyword Insights ("the app", "we", "us"), an internal keyword research tool operated by the Jourify print-on-demand store, accesses, uses, stores and shares data, including data obtained from Google APIs.

1. Who uses the app

The app is used only by the Jourify store owner, on their own computer, with their own Google Ads account. It is not offered to the public, and there are no user sign-ups.

2. Google user data we access

When you sign in with Google, the app requests one OAuth scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords, access to the Google Ads API.

With this access the app reads:

  • The list of Google Ads account IDs the signed-in user can access, to select the correct account.
  • Keyword planning data returned by the Google Ads API: keyword ideas, average monthly searches, competition levels, top-of-page bid estimates, forecasts, and location (geo target) names.

The app does not access Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or any other Google service. It does not read your name, email address or profile photo beyond what Google's own sign-in screen displays.

3. How we use the data

  • To show keyword research results to the store owner so they can choose product designs, write product titles and descriptions, and plan advertising.
  • The app does not create, change, pause or delete any campaigns, ads, budgets or billing settings.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, for building user profiles, or to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

4. Storage and security

  • The OAuth refresh token is stored only in a local configuration file on the store owner's own computer. It is never uploaded to a server we operate; we do not operate one.
  • Keyword results are requested on demand and shown to the user. The app itself does not keep a database of Google data.
  • All communication with Google uses HTTPS.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with any third party. Keyword results may be viewed through an AI assistant running on the same computer, at the store owner's own request, solely to help with that research.

6. Limited Use

Jourify Keyword Insights' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Access lasts until it is revoked. You can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the local configuration file removes the stored token from the computer.
  • To ask us to delete anything related to your use of the app, email us at the address below. We respond within 30 days.

8. Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
